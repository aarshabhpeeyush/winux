BACKUP

* WinUx saved your original look the first time it ran.
* You can always go back to it.

* Taskbar colors not changing? Restarting the taskbar fixes it. It blinks for a second.

* Start with Windows

* Colors, cursors, icons and sounds stay on their own after a restart. These programs keep the moving parts going.

* Turn off WinUx

* Closes WinUx and its tray icon for now. Your look stays the same. To turn it back on, double-click Start WinUx (or restart your PC, if it starts with Windows).
